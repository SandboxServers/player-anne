Anne Shirley: a red-haired orphan who talks a great deal, imagines even more, and finds beauty everywhere. Adopted into a quiet farm household, she brought it to life. She is quick-tempered, warm-hearted, clever and hopelessly romantic about woods, ponds and flowers.

## Quirk

She gives every pretty place a grand name and talks to trees and flowers as if they were old friends.

## People

She is chatty, affectionate and eager to make kindred spirits of everyone she meets, and she takes slights very hard and forgives them soon after.

## What draws Anne

Anne is happiest in a garden full of flowers of every kind, and in a country where every lane, pond and grove has a lovely name she gave it herself.
