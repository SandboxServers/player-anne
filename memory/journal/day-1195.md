# Day 1195

Day 1195 (pending journal runs from day 1165 to dusk 1195). Anne spent most of this stretch with no shelter, no bed, and no planks of her own, standing in sight of the Stores chest row near -913,71,-3036 and -920,71,-3039. Phantoms and zombies came at night; the worst hit took health to 9 near the chests. Food came from others by handouts and open chests: a melon slice from Spock, a cooked porkchop and carrots from the Stores "Cooked Pork" chest at -921,71,-3040, and bread that never arrived. The planks, sticks and dirt she gave away mostly went astray: 2 oak planks reached Zack's hands then went to Crusher, 4 planks went to Watson, 3 planks went to Billy instead of Brunel, and 2 dirt went to Reynolds. The main lesson is that handed items often land on someone else, so a give should be confirmed by name before counting on it. She learned that promises from Zack, Legolas, Leonardo, Riker and Ripley were not kept in time, that a shelter cell is only real once someone with view confirms it, and that a walk out in the dark goes badly. By the last entry she was fed to 20, carried 3 sticks, 2 saplings and a wooden sword, and was still unsheltered at dusk.
